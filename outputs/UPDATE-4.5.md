# NEON//NULL 4.5

Larger single-player monsters award a size bonus on top of their existing scrap reward: one extra scrap per six radius units above radius12. Small enemies keep their base reward.

Standard Endless now banks exactly the nonnegative whole-number run scrap balance remaining on defeat, once per run. Purchases and upgrades reduce that balance. The former score/time formula, 60-second requirement and 120-scrap payout cap no longer apply to standard Endless. The HUD and results display the remaining balance. Restarting or leaving early does not award defeat rewards. Creative and Practice do not bank rewards.

Online co-op retains its separate server-authoritative reward rules from MULTIPLAYER-SETUP.md; this change applies to the single-player Endless run wallet.
