# NEON//NULL 4.7

Ordinary ammo crates and boss ammo caches restore eight times their previous ammunition, subject to existing magazine and reserve limits. The ordinary single-player ammo-drop chance is now 8.05% per eligible kill (7% multiplied by 1.15). Supply caches, purchased restocks, Ammo Cache utility and healing drop rates are unchanged. Active reloads are not cancelled by pickups.

Online co-op ammo pickups restore eight magazines of reserve per ranged weapon instead of one. Ordinary enemy ammo probability rises from6.6% to7.59%, preserving the5.4% heal probability and guaranteed boss pickup behavior. Deploy the updated backend to apply co-op changes.
